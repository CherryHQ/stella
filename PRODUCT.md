# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Non-technical users who primarily chat with their AI assistant. They open the app daily for conversation, occasionally adjust agent settings, skills, or automations. They value clarity over configurability and expect things to "just work" without reading docs. Context: personal device, relaxed attention, variable screen sizes.

## Product Purpose

Stella is a self-hosted AI assistant with persistent memory. The web UI is the primary interface for chatting, managing agents, configuring skills and automations, and reviewing task history. Success: the user feels like they have a personal, intelligent companion that remembers context and handles recurring work without friction.

## Brand Personality

Playful, smart, alive. The interface has opinions and personality without being loud. It feels modern and curated, like someone designed every detail on purpose. Confident enough to make bold choices, warm enough to feel personal rather than clinical.

## Anti-references

- Slack: too noisy, too many channels, notification overload, messaging-app anxiety
- ChatGPT: generic centered-chat emptiness, corporate AI product sameness, suggestion-chip clutter
- Enterprise dashboards: data tables, grey-on-grey, configuration fatigue, admin-panel energy

## Design Principles

1. **Conversation is king.** Every layout decision serves the chat. Configuration panels support the conversation; they never compete with it.
2. **Opinionated defaults.** Reduce choices. Show the right thing at the right time rather than exposing every option simultaneously.
3. **Alive, not animated.** The UI should feel responsive and present, not decorated with gratuitous motion. Transitions acknowledge user action; nothing moves without reason.
4. **Personal, not precious.** Warmth over sterility. The app should feel like yours, not like enterprise software you were assigned.
5. **Progressive disclosure.** Simple surface, depth on demand. Non-technical users see what they need; power features reveal themselves contextually.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Best-effort: maintain reasonable contrast ratios, support keyboard navigation for core flows, don't rely solely on color to convey state. No formal WCAG compliance target.

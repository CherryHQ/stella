---
title: Research Workflows
---

Recally is most useful when reading is continuous.

## Track an industry

Subscribe to feeds from companies, blogs, standards bodies, and analysts. Use digests to see what changed without reading every post.

Workflow:

- Add RSS feeds for trusted sources.
- Star high-signal articles.
- Ask Recally to summarize weekly changes.
- Chat with important articles to extract implications.

## Research a topic

Save web pages and PDFs into a topic-focused reading list.

Workflow:

- Save source material as you find it.
- Tag articles by topic.
- Ask for summaries and comparisons.
- Chat with individual articles for deeper understanding.
- Generate a digest for your team.

## Monitor policies or releases

Use Recally for sources that change over time: policy pages, changelogs, release notes, and technical blogs.

Workflow:

- Subscribe to feeds where available.
- Save important pages manually.
- Ask for "what changed" summaries.
- Turn important updates into agent tasks.

## Move from reading to action

Reading should produce decisions or work. When an article matters, ask Stella to create follow-up tasks:

> Turn this article into a briefing for the Finance agent and create review tasks for policy changes.

Recally keeps attention organized; agents turn attention into work.
